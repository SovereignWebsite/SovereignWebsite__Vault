Sovereign Agentic Website

Your website, from a vault.

A site sealed in an encrypted sgit vault opens here, in your own browser. You can open it from a link with the key after the #, a QR code (scanned with your phone camera, or dropped here as an image), or a vault zip and its key. This page decrypts the vault on your device. The key is never sent anywhere, and the site runs in a sandboxed frame that cannot reach the network.

On this device

Vaults you open are listed here, so you only bring the key once: this browser keeps the key encrypted with its own key pair, made for this page, and never the decrypted site. None yet.

    How it works

    A vault zip is what sgit vault backup writes: the vault's encrypted objects and a small manifest with its id, and no key. With the read key, this page decrypts the newest commit with AES-256-GCM, rebuilds the files, and shows them. A vault with a designs/ folder shows each design one tap apart. Nothing is uploaded from the browser.
